# Integration and workflow capability matrix

<!-- section:contract -->
## Contract

This matrix distinguishes source implementation, source-run evidence, source GUI/worker acceptance, and clean distribution acceptance. Nine migrated research implementations have been exercised from the source checkout, including the five GUI courses, the 19-figure report, cache, and MCP paths. This does not certify a clean wheel installation or optional runtimes in every user's environment. “Discoverable” means a capability can be listed, not that a calculation succeeded.

All example results are RESEARCH-ONLY with `pit_guarantee=false`. Inputs are explicit user-owned CSV, Parquet, or completed manifests; no raw J-Quants cache, full report, model artifact, credential, or Paper ledger is bundled. The accepted reference workflow is described in the [five GUI research courses](GUI_RESEARCH_COURSES.md); screenshot provenance and scope are in the [public evidence summary](../demos/research-20261006/EVIDENCE_SUMMARY.md).

## Reading the capability states

Source-checkout GUI/worker paths have been exercised, but that is separate from clean wheel/package acceptance. Across four optional runtime groups, 31 source workflow tests passed with no skips; these were local checks, not GitHub Actions runs. A discovered provider or listed engine is not proof of a successful calculation. Broker read-only diagnostics are explicit and separate from the zero-network offline preview; neither means a real terminal was validated. Nothing here upgrades the research outputs to strict PIT, investable, PAPER-READY, or live-trading evidence.

## Next work

The [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) tracks the remaining clean wheel/install gate and longer-horizon research requirements. The most important research gaps are reliable point-in-time availability, exchange-calendar and corporate-action handling, dividends, capacity and realistic execution costs, and fresh forward observation. Public Regime remains Off; its private state-machine bridge is not included.
